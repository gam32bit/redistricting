Explore WJCC Redistricting Survey Responses

Search what people wrote in the Williamsburg-James City County school division's redistricting survey conducted from May 12 to June 7, 2026. Type a word in the box to see each response that uses it, and use the filters to narrow to a group of respondents, a school, or one of the five questions. Click a day on the response timeline — or drag across several — to keep only the responses from those days.

1,374 of the survey's 1,540 respondents wrote something in at least one of the five free-text questions, and those are the responses you can read here.

How this page was built

This page was built with AI assistance. You can read my blog post for a full explainer of how I used Claude Code for this project. AI can make mistakes. I designed this page so that none of it depends on AI interpretation of the text or data. All the responses are lifted verbatim from the division's PDF by a traditional coding script. Readers can verify by downloading the source PDF.

The division made redactions on some responses before releasing the PDF. Those redactions show up as blank spaces in the responses below. For example, “getting 2 years at , then having to do 1 year at.”
