I'd heard the redistricting survey results would be posted at the end of June, and they
were: one PDF, 3,106 pages.

Inside it are 1,540 responses. Most of the questions are multiple choice, but five of them
let people write whatever they wanted, and 1,374 people wrote something in at least one.
It's the most direct record we have of what people here actually think about redrawing
school boundaries.

It is also 3,106 pages. I wanted to know what people said about buses, or about Ford's
Colony, or about diversity, and there's no good way to ask a PDF that kind of question. So
I put together a page that lets you ask it.

**[Search the redistricting survey →](https://redistricting.jwcaterine.com/)**

Type a word and you get every response that uses it, with the word highlighted. You can
narrow to parents, or staff, or a single school, or one of the five questions, or just the
days a response came in. One tab lists every single mention of your word in context, and
another counts every word people used — so you can see what came up most without taking my
word for it.

A few things worth knowing. The text is verbatim, typos and all. Nothing is summarized or
ranked; search is literal, and the same word always returns the same responses. I built the
page with AI assistance, which is disclosed on it. And one quirk worth mentioning: the
division's PDF has a font problem that silently drops every *f*, *ff*, *fi* and *fl* when you copy
text out of it, which is why a straight copy-paste turns Lafayette into "La ayette." My copy
puts those letters back.

The survey was open to anyone who wanted to answer, so it isn't a representative sample of
the county and was never meant to be read as a vote. The division's original PDF is linked
at the bottom of the search page, and if anything I show looks wrong, that document is the
authority.

Hope it's useful to anyone else following this.
